My 2026 beach visits

I prefer beaches over mountains, though I love both equally. The feeling of a cool breeze, a beautiful sunset with the sky filled with orange, yellow, pink and blue, a good playlist, and waves softly hitting you as they erode the sand beneath your feet is just heaven.

All the beaches at the places I visited had their own beauty, but if I had to rank them based on beauty and cleanliness, Cabo de Rama Beach in Goa would win.

Cabo de Rama Beach, Goa

I felt as though I was abroad, and the time I spent there was so memorable. The water was crystal clear, but it was deep enough to make entering it very risky if you don't know how to swim. The view is just so exotic, and not many people visit this beach. The beach is at the bottom, and when you see it from the top, I literally felt like jumping down. Haha, I know that's foolish, but that's how breathtaking it is.

As you walk down the steps, your energy and senses just won't calm down because you've witnessed one of the best beaches in India from above. There's a small section of water to the side if you want to enjoy the beach as a non-swimmer. There are also some fancy items at the top for aesthetic pictures.

Kerala

Next would be the beaches in Kerala. I visited Golden Sand Beach. Though we weren't allowed to get into the water, the beach looked majestic, with golden sand, rough and tough waves hitting the shore, and a cool breeze. I visited the beach as part of the Poovar backwater cruise.

The ticket was expensive for a one-hour boat ride, but the mangroves, backwaters and beach made it all worth it. There is a resort connected to it, with floating rooms, restaurants and vendors selling tender coconuts on boats. We didn't go there because we were running late.

I also visited Kovalam Beach when we visited Thiruvananthapuram.

Calicut

The other picture is from Calicut. The beach is calmest between 3 and 4 p.m. After that, it gets packed with crowds. There are lots of stalls and fun activities on the beach.

Mumbai

Lastly, Mumbai. I visited that beach after a long time. I usually used to visit Juhu Beach every time I went to Mumbai. Surprisingly, Versova is much cleaner than Juhu. I understand the situation at Juhu, but it is disturbing when you visit the beach in the early mornings or evenings.

There are food stalls here as well. The road was under repair, and construction was underway on a road that would make travel easier and faster.
