Out of office · into the blue · 2026

Travel diaries.

Places, coastlines and little moments I want to keep.

I have always preferred beaches over mountains, although I love both equally. Over the years, I've visited many beaches, and somehow, I still never seem to get over them. Every beach has something different to offer, and no matter how many I visit, the excitement of seeing the sea never really fades.

There is something about the beach that makes me forget everything else. The cool breeze, a beautiful sunset painting the sky in shades of orange, yellow, pink and blue, a good playlist playing in the background, and waves gently brushing against your feet as they reshape the sand beneath them. Honestly, heaven has its own little address!

In 2026, I got to visit beaches across Mumbai, Goa and Kerala. Each had its own character, charm and experience. But if I had to rank them based on beauty and cleanliness, one beach would undoubtedly take the crown.

01 · Cabo de Rama Beach, Goa

My absolute favourite!

Cabo de Rama Beach was easily the highlight of my beach visits this year. When I first saw it from the top, I genuinely felt as though I had landed somewhere abroad. The crystal-clear water, the beautiful coastline and the relatively fewer visitors made the entire place feel exotic.

The beach sits below a viewpoint, and you have to make your way down a flight of steps to reach it. But the moment you see that stretch of blue from above, your excitement just keeps building with every step. I literally felt like jumping straight down (haha! Thankfully, I had enough sense not to).

The water is incredibly clear, but don't let its inviting appearance fool you. It gets deep, and entering the sea can be risky if you aren't a confident swimmer. There is also a smaller section of water along the side where non-swimmers can enjoy the surroundings more safely, while still exercising caution.

There are a few little spots and fancy decorative elements at the top that make for some lovely aesthetic pictures too. What I loved most was how untouched and peaceful the place felt. It wasn't crowded, the surroundings were beautiful, and for those few hours, I could have easily forgotten I was still in India.

If there is one beach I would happily revisit, this is it!

02 · Kerala: Golden Sand Beach and Kovalam

Kerala had a different kind of coastal beauty to offer.

I visited Golden Sand Beach as part of the Poovar backwater cruise. The boat ride itself was quite expensive for an hour, but the experience made up for it. The mangroves, backwaters and eventually the beach made the entire journey worthwhile.

We weren't allowed to enter the water when we visited, but that didn't take away from the experience. The golden sand, powerful waves crashing against the shore and the cool breeze made the beach look absolutely majestic.

There was also a resort connected to this stretch, with floating rooms, restaurants and even vendors selling tender coconuts from boats! That was something I found particularly interesting. Unfortunately, we were running late and couldn't explore that part.

I also visited Kovalam Beach during our trip to Thiruvananthapuram. Kerala's coastline really does have its own personality, and I would love to explore more of it.

03 · Calicut Beach: The Afternoon Calm

This photograph is from Calicut Beach, which I happened to visit during the afternoon, around 3–4 PM.

It was relatively calm at that time, with the waves rolling in and plenty of space to enjoy the view. But apparently, the beach has a completely different personality later in the evening! After that, it gets packed with people. There are plenty of food stalls and fun activities along the beach, making it a lively place to spend an evening.

I quite enjoyed seeing this quieter side of it. Sometimes, the best way to experience a place is to visit it before the crowds arrive.

04 · Mumbai: Versova over Juhu?

A little Mumbai nostalgia!

I visited a Mumbai beach after quite some time. Whenever I used to visit Mumbai, Juhu Beach was usually my regular stop. This time, however, I visited Versova, and surprisingly, I found it considerably cleaner than Juhu.

I understand that Juhu faces its own challenges, given the number of people visiting it, but seeing the condition of the beach, especially during early mornings or evenings, can be quite disappointing.

Versova had food stalls too, so there was still plenty to explore. The road nearby was undergoing repairs and construction, with improvements intended to make travelling easier and faster.

It was nice to revisit Mumbai's coastline, but this time discover a different beach instead of returning to my usual one.

A little more love for the waves

Every beach had something different to offer. Some gave me crystal-clear water and breathtaking views, some had golden sand and powerful waves, while others offered the simple pleasure of sitting by the shore and watching the world go by.

But Cabo de Rama definitely won my personal ranking this year!

And, of course, a special mention to the person who made these little adventures possible. Funded by Dad. ❤️

Here's to more sunsets, sandy feet, seashells, spontaneous trips and many more beaches to cross off the list!
