Tomato & Herb Pasta

A quick, comforting pasta with garlic, tomatoes, olive oil and fresh herbs. Sample recipe for editing.

Open ingredients & method

Ingredients

  • 200 g pasta
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Fresh basil or coriander, salt and pepper

Method

  1. Cook pasta in salted water and reserve a little pasta water.
  2. Warm olive oil, soften garlic, then add tomatoes and season.
  3. Toss pasta through the sauce with a splash of pasta water.
  4. Finish with herbs. Adjust seasoning to taste.

Vanilla Tea Cake

A simple soft tea cake, included as a sample baking entry. Replace with your own version.

Open ingredients & method

Ingredients

  • 1½ cups flour
  • ¾ cup sugar
  • ½ cup softened butter
  • 2 eggs
  • ½ cup milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla and 1 tsp baking powder

Method

  1. Heat oven to 175°C and grease a small cake tin.
  2. Cream butter and sugar, then beat in eggs and vanilla.
  3. Fold in flour and baking powder, alternating with milk.
  4. Bake for 30 to 35 minutes, until a skewer comes out clean.

Banana Oat Pancakes

A small-stack breakfast idea for the sample recipe shelf.

Open ingredients & method

Ingredients

  • 1 ripe banana
  • ½ cup oats, ground
  • 1 egg
  • ¼ cup milk
  • ½ tsp baking powder and a pinch of cinnamon

Method

  1. Mash banana and whisk with egg and milk.
  2. Stir in ground oats, baking powder and cinnamon.
  3. Cook small pancakes on a lightly greased pan over medium-low heat.
  4. Serve with fruit or honey.

DEMO RECIPE · 30 MIN · COMFORT FOOD

Roasted Pumpkin Soup

A cosy soup placeholder with a short ingredient list and an easy method.

Open ingredients & method

Ingredients

  • 2 cups pumpkin cubes
  • ½ onion, sliced
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1½ cups vegetable stock
  • 1 tsp olive oil, salt and pepper

Method

  1. Roast pumpkin, onion and garlic with oil at 200°C until tender.
  2. Blend with warm stock until smooth.
  3. Simmer briefly and season to taste.
  4. Serve warm with toasted bread.
